    Paper and pen setup

    Tape an A4 sheet sideways in front of the robot, near edge about 17 cm from the base. Use a felt-tip pen wrapped with tape where the gripper holds it (about 12 mm thick). Draw it on paper starts the drawing program: it opens the gripper for the pen, then you place the pen on the paper by hand (or press Auto), and your drawing starts by itself.
