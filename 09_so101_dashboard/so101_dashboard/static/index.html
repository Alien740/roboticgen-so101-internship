    Draw with a pen step by step – the robot holds a pen and draws on an A4 sheet

    1. Tape an A4 sheet down sideways (landscape), centred in front of the robot, its near edge about 17 cm from the base (the drawing spot is 28 cm out).
    2. Have a felt-tip pen ready, cap off.
    3. Speed doesn't matter – drawing uses its own slow, careful speed.
    4. Press Start drawing. The robot parks, then opens its gripper for the pen.
    5. After it grips the pen, the arm goes limp and you place the pen on the paper by hand – that spot is where it draws. Then pick a shape or sketch your own.

      Your upper arm, elbow and hand drive the robot's shoulder, elbow and wrist in one vertical plane
          (base fixed at the slider, + = the robot's right). When you tick Follow, your current arm shape is tied to the
          robot's current pose and only your movements from there move it, so it never jumps. Each joint stays in a
          safe range and the gripper never goes below the table. It finds which way you face and which arm is nearer
          the camera by itself.
