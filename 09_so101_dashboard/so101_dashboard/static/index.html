    The box on the video is the robot's reach: keep your hand inside it. Only hand tracking is used
          (the most precise model). Following first moves the robot to a ready pose above the table, then eases into
          your hand's position.
    
      
    

      
    
        
    How to move stand or sit SIDEWAYS to the camera, so it sees your arm in profile
    
        
    
          
    raise your upper arm forward / lower itrobot shoulder leans out / back
    
          
    straighten your elbow / bend it (forearm up)robot forearm goes down / up
    
          
    bend your hand down / upgripper points down / up
    
          
    curl your fingers into a fist / open themgripper closes fully / opens
    
          
    Base direction sliderturns the plane the robot works in
    
          
    arm leaves the picturerobot holds still
    
          
    Space / EscE-STOP (always)
    
        
    
        
    Your upper arm, elbow and hand drive the robot's shoulder, elbow and wrist in one vertical plane
          (base fixed at the slider, + = the robot's right). When you tick Follow, your current arm shape is tied to the
          robot's current pose and only your movements from there move it, so it never jumps. Each joint stays in a
          safe range and the gripper never goes below the table. It finds which way you face and which arm is nearer
          the camera by itself.

          your own arm lengths. The gripper stops at the edge of reach and never goes below the table.
