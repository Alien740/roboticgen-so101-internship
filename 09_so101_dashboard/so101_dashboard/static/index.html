    Draw with a pen runs the framework's tested drawing procedure

    1. Tape paper down 22–34 cm in front of the robot; a felt-tip pen, cap off. Speed doesn't matter here.
    2. Start drawing – the dashboard parks and hands the arm to the drawing procedure.
    3. Load: lay the pen ACROSS the jaws like a cigarette, tip out 5–7 cm → go; tug-test → go.
    4. Tilt: does the tip point straight down? go / roll ±10 / flip.
    5. Height: type how high the tip is above the paper (mm), then jog down until it just touches.
    6. Draw: Level paper once, then pick a shape. Finish hands the pen back.

    Your upper arm, elbow and hand drive the robot's shoulder, elbow and wrist in one vertical plane
          (base fixed at the slider, + = the robot's right). When you tick Follow, your current arm shape is tied to the
          robot's current pose and only your movements from there move it, so it never jumps. Each joint stays in a
          safe range and the gripper never goes below the table. It finds which way you face and which arm is nearer
          the camera by itself.
